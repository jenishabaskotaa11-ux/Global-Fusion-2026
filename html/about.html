OUR STORY • ACADEMIC COLLABORATION

BRINGING THE WORLD TO KATHMANDU

“One Campus. Many Cultures. One Global Experience.”
An immersive festival where student creativity bridges geographical boundaries.

INSTITUTIONAL PARTNERSHIP

LBEF College & Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU)

Established in 1998, Lord Buddha Education Foundation (LBEF) stands proudly as the First IT College of Nepal, having nurtured over 12,000 technology and management professionals.

In longstanding academic collaboration with Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU, Malaysia) — one of Malaysia's premier Premier Digital Tech Institutions hosting students from over 130 countries — this festival represents the zenith of cross-border collegiate solidarity.

Note: Global Fusion 2026 is a creative fictional student demonstration project crafted for front-end web development academic requirements.
🇳🇵 Kathmandu, Nepal • 🇲🇾 Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
CORE PILLARS

WHAT DRIVES GLOBAL FUSION

Four guiding philosophies shaping every exhibition, pavilion, and performance on campus.

🌍

Multicultural Empathy

Transforming textbook geography into living sensory experiences through authentic food, native instruments, and respectful traditional folklore.

💡

Tech & Heritage Fusion

Leveraging digital innovation, digital passports, UI/UX preservation hackathons, and virtual broadcasting to celebrate ancestral roots.

🤝

Student Leadership

100% student-curated and executed. From culinary chefs to stage sound engineers, youth leadership is the beating heart of Global Fusion.

🕊️

Unity In Diversity

Creating a safe, inclusive festival ground where differences in language, faith, and customs become celebrations of collective humanity.
