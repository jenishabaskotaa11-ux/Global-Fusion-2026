IMMERSIVE CAMPUS EXPERIENCES

ACTIVITIES & EXPERIENCES

From hands-on batik waxing to lively polyglot language cafes and electrifying night concerts, every corner of campus offers an adventure.

Culinary

Global Food Fair

Savor street snacks across 50 international stalls operated by student culinary delegations. From fresh Nepalese momos to spicy Mexican churros and Malaysian satay.

📍 Food Street Concourse Schedule →
Fashion

International Fashion Show

An elegant runway presentation of royal textiles, handcrafted Hanboks, Dhaka topis, Banarasi sarees, Gele wraps, and modern eco-fusion student garments.

📍 Main Runway Stage Schedule →
Dance

Cultural Dance Showcase

Featuring heart-racing Indian Kathak spins, the mystical demon-god Newari Lakhey dance, Brazilian Samba, and Balinese Kecak rhythmic chanting.

📍 Main Arena Schedule →
Live Music

World Music Stage

Acoustic fusion concerts connecting Nepali Sarangi, Malaysian Gamelan bells, Japanese Wadaiko drums, and soulful West African Djembe rhythms.

📍 Sunset Acoustic Shell Schedule →
Language

Language Exchange Polyglot Cafe

Grab a complimentary herbal tea and learn essential conversational phrases in Nepali, Japanese, Bahasa Melayu, Spanish, French, Korean, and Arabic.

📍 Campus Library Courtyard Schedule →
Fine Arts

Cultural Art Exhibition

Admire student fine arts including traditional Paubha and Thangka scroll paintings, Chinese calligraphy scrolls, and indigenous woodcarving displays.

📍 Innovation Gallery Hall Schedule →
Games

Traditional Games Arena

Participate in spirited historical friendly games: Malaysian Congkak, Nepalese Dandi Biyo, Japanese Kendama, and Brazilian Capoeira movement circles.

📍 North Sports Ground Schedule →
Networking

International Networking Night

Connect with exchange scholars, international student ambassadors, faculty researchers, and industry mentors over multicultural hors d'oeuvres.

📍 Global Village Sky Lounge Schedule →
Oral Lore

Storytelling Around the World

Gather around dusk firepits to hear oral folklore: West African Anansi trickster tales, Himalayan Yeti myths, and Mayan celestial constellation legends.

📍 Amphitheater Hearth Schedule →
Hands-On

Master Crafts & Workshops

Take part in guided hands-on craft sessions: Malaysian canting wax batik, Japanese origami folding, Henna mehndi application, and Djembe drumming basics.
