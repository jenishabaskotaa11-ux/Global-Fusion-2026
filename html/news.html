Teej Celebration Brings Traditional Music and Colour to LBEF

LBEF celebrated Teej with traditional red attire, songs, dances and activities including musical chairs and a mehndi competition. A shared lunch rounded off the occasion, bringing colleagues together through Nepal’s living cultural traditions. Source: LBEF Connect, Vol. 8, Issue 3.

December 2023 • 2 min read

LBEF Highlights Yomari Punhi and Nepal’s Food Heritage

LBEF’s festival message highlighted Udhauli Parva and Yomari Punhi, recognising traditions that connect food, faith and community. Its description of Yomari Punhi honoured Annapurna, the deity of food—a fitting reminder of the cultural stories behind every festival dish. Source: LBEF College’s official festival post.

Student Representatives Take a Step Towards Humanity

LBEF’s Student Representative Council organised “A Step Towards Humanity” around Saraswati Puja. Members visited Pranavananda Ashram Nepal in Guheshwori and donated stationery, supported by contributions from the college community. Source: LBEF Connect, Vol. 7, Issue 1.

LBEF Business Quiz Brings Students Together Across Programmes

Six teams from LBEF’s business and technology programmes took part in a business quiz featuring five rounds and 50 questions. The competition encouraged general knowledge and awareness of the corporate world, with participation certificates presented the following month. Source: LBEF Connect, Vol. 2, Issue 1.

Holi Celebrations Bring the LBEF Community Together

LBEF’s campus newsletter recorded a Holi celebration on the E-Block grounds, bringing students, staff and faculty together for the festival of colours.
