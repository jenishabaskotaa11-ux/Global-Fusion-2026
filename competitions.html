NRs 350,000 PRIZE POOL

FESTIVAL COMPETITIONS

Compete, collaborate, and leave your mark! Open to collegiate teams from LBEF, APU Malaysia, and visiting international delegations.

NRs 75,000 PRIZE
GASTRONOMY BATTLE 👥 2–3 Chefs per Team

International Cooking Challenge (Global Culinary Clash)

Student culinary duos are challenged to prepare a three-course tasting banquet fusing traditional Himalayan spices with international culinary techniques (Southeast Asian, Mediterranean, or Mesoamerican).

Eligibility: Enrolled Undergraduate Students
Registration Deadline: 25 Nov 2026
Live Kitchen Round: 4 Dec 2026 (Day 2)
Judging Panel: Executive Chefs & Food Critics
Click to View Competition Rules & Judging Criteria ▾

1. Teams must use at least 2 indigenous Nepalese ingredients (e.g. Jimbu, Timur, Dalle Khursani) and 1 international component.

2. Prep & cooking window is strictly 90 minutes followed by 10 minutes of plating.

3. Scoring Rubric: Taste & Balance (40%), Cultural Fusion Concept (30%), Plating Presentation (20%), Kitchen Hygiene (10%).

🏆 1st: NRs 50k • 2nd: NRs 25k Register Team For Culinary Clash →
NRs 100,000 PRIZE
PERFORMANCE CHAMPIONSHIP 👥 4–12 Dancers per Troupe

World Talent Showcase: Folk Dance Championship

A high-energy choreography competition on the festival amphitheater stage. Featuring South Asian folk, ASEAN court dances, African tribal polyrhythms, Latin salsa/capoeira, and East Asian fan dances.

Eligibility: College Troupes & Cultural Societies
Registration Deadline: 22 Nov 2026
Final Stage Round: 5 Dec 2026 (Day 3)
Judging Panel: National Folk Choreographers
Click to View Competition Rules & Judging Criteria ▾

1. Routine duration must be between 6 and 10 minutes maximum.

2. Costumes must represent genuine cultural heritage and adhere to stage safety guidelines.

3. Scoring Rubric: Authenticity & Technique (35%), Synchronization & Energy (30%), Costume Fidelity (20%), Audience Impact (15%).

🏆 1st: NRs 60k • 2nd: NRs 40k Register Troupe For Dance →
NRs 85,000 PRIZE
TECH & HERITAGE HACKATHON 👥 2–4 Coders/Designers

Cultural UI/UX & Web Heritage Hackathon

In honor of the LBEF & APU IT collaboration, students build functional front-end web applications preserving indigenous languages, gamifying cultural learning, or building digital travel passports.

Eligibility: IT, Computing, & Design Students
Submission Deadline: 28 Nov 2026
Final Presentation: 4 Dec 2026 (Day 2)
Tech Stack: HTML5, CSS3, Modern JavaScript
Click to View Hackathon Guidelines & Criteria ▾

1. Solutions must be responsive and accessible across mobile and desktop.

2. Open source frameworks or pure vanilla stacks permitted; code must be hosted on GitHub.

3. Scoring Rubric: UX Polish & Aesthetics (35%), Cultural Relevance & Utility (30%), Code Architecture (20%), Live Demo (15%).

🏆 1st: NRs 50k • 2nd: NRs 35k Register Hackathon Team →
NRs 90,000 PRIZE
VISUAL ARTS 👥 Solo Submissions

“My Culture Through My Lens” Photography & Film

Submit original high-resolution photo essays or 3-minute mini-documentaries capturing living heritage, artisanal crafts, elders, or festival rituals from your homeland.

Eligibility: Open to All Student Photographers
Submission Deadline: 26 Nov 2026
Gallery Exhibition: Days 1–3 at Innovation Hall
Judging Panel: Photojournalists & Film Directors
Click to View Photo/Film Rules & Criteria ▾

1. Photos must be minimum 3000px on long edge, accompanied by a 150-word cultural narrative.

2. Mini-docs must not exceed 180 seconds, with English subtitles where dialogue is present.

3. Scoring Rubric: Narrative Depth (40%), Composition & Lighting (35%), Emotional Impact (25%).

🏆 Photo: NRs 45k • Film: NRs 45k Submit Photo Entry →
